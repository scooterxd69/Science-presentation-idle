SCENE 06 · SCIENCE

The Scientific Principle

Energy, transferred
through empty space.

Faraday's Law

A changing magnetic field induces an electromotive force in a nearby coil.

Electromagnetic Induction

Alternating current in the transmitter coil generates a fluctuating field.

Magnetic Flux

Flux linking the receiver coil drives the induced current that charges the battery.

Resonance

Matched resonant frequencies between coils maximize transfer efficiency.

Mutual Inductance

Coupling between transmitter and receiver coils defines how much power transfers.
